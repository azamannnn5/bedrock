Lapidary Equipment & Supplies for Cutting, Grinding and Polishing

Bedrock Lapidary supplies the equipment behind every stage of stone and glass work: lapidary saws and diamond blades to cut rough, cabbing machines, grinders, wheels and sanders to shape it, flat lap machines to flatten and polish it, and rock tumblers to finish it. Stock up on tumbler grit and polish, diamond compound and dop wax, or browse glass grinders and lathes, tools and rockhounding books.

Not sure where to start? Our buying guides explain how to choose a saw, a tumbler, a cabbing machine or a flat lap for the work you actually do.
