Policies

Manufacturer's Warranties

Warranty length and terms vary by product and manufacturer. The warranty for each item is listed on its product page under the Specifications tab.

How warranty claims work

If a machine develops a fault covered by its manufacturer's warranty, contact us first rather than the manufacturer directly. We handle the claim on your behalf and coordinate replacement parts or repair.

  • Email contact@bedrocklapidary.com with your order number, the issue, and photos or video if the problem is mechanical.
  • Most claims are reviewed within 2 business days.
  • Normal wear items (blades, belts, wheels, barrels, and similar consumables) are not covered under mechanical warranty.
Within 30 days of purchase? If your order is still within its 30-day window, our standard return policy may be simpler and faster than a warranty claim.

Typical warranty terms

Most machines we carry ship with a 1-year manufacturer warranty against defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. Some models carry 2-year terms, and units used in salt-water environments are generally limited to a 90-day warranty due to accelerated corrosion. Check the specific product page for exact terms before purchase.
